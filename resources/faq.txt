How does the compatibility estimate work?
The overall score is the lowest of the scores for graphics, processor, RAM, drive type and free space. Available VRAM also affects the verdict.

How do I choose a drive?
Click Detect hardware, then select the drive where you plan to store the files. You can also enter the drive type and free space manually.

What data does the app send?
Hardware detection takes place on your PC and starts only after you give permission. The app contacts gta6recomp.com to retrieve news, articles and release status.

What is Recomp Hub?
Recomp Hub is an independent Windows app for following GTA 6 recompilation news and estimating PC compatibility. The Projects page shows progress reported by the source site.
